{{ if $zh }}招生要求{{ else }}Admission requirements{{ end }}

  1. {{ if $zh }}满足香港科技大学（广州）的基本招生要求。{{ else }}Meet the general admission requirements of The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou).{{ end }}
  2. {{ if $zh }}具有本科及以上学历，具有电子、微电子、集成电路、计算机或其他相关专业背景，并满足学校的英语入学要求。{{ else }}Hold a bachelor’s degree or above in electronics, microelectronics, integrated circuits, computer science or engineering, or another related discipline, and satisfy the University’s English-language admission requirements.{{ end }}
  3. {{ if $zh }}具有芯片设计、片上网络设计、神经网络硬件加速器设计或计算机体系结构研究经验者将获优先考虑。{{ else }}Applicants with research experience in chip design, Network-on-Chip design, neural-network hardware accelerators, or computer architecture will be given preference.{{ end }}
  4. {{ if $zh }}优秀学生可以申请每年人民币 18 万元的全额奖学金（全日制博士研究生每月人民币 15,000 元，最长 4 年）。{{ else }}Outstanding students may apply for a full scholarship of CNY 180,000 per year (CNY 15,000 per month, for up to four years for full-time PhD students).{{ end }}
