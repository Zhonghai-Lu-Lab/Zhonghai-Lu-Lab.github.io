summary: Addressing cross-die network composability challenges in heterogeneous NoC-based chiplet systems through topology, routing, end-to-end flow control, and Quality-of-Service analysis.
abstract: Chiplet integration turns the interconnection of Network-on-Chip (NoC)-based dies into a network-composition problem. Even when each chiplet network is correct and well-performing in isolation, composition may fail to preserve global correctness or predictable, scalable performance. This project studies composability across topology, routing, end-to-end flow control, and Quality of Service (QoS), and investigates cross-layer coordination and an open, standardized protocol stack for interoperable chiplet networks.
