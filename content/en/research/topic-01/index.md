This direction explores **Computational Network-on-Chip (cNoC)** as a new computing architecture. It moves beyond the conventional von Neumann model—and differs from Compute-in-Memory—by enabling efficient computation while data is in transit across the on-chip network.

The research combines scalable on-chip communication with computation-aware routing and architecture design, turning the network itself into an active part of the computing system.
